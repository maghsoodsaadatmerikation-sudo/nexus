# NEXUS v2.1 — Capability Enforcement Boundary

Status: ENGINEERING CONTRACT / NOT SEALED

## Purpose

v2.1 hardens the v2.0 capability primitive by moving capability validation to the constitutional authorization boundary and by closing validity-window and audit gaps without rewriting the historical v2.0 seal.

## Constitutional invariant

`A_child <= A_parent <= A_in`

Evidence remains non-authoritative. A capability grant may reduce or preserve authority but may never create authority.

## Required boundary behavior

A delegated request may become `AuthorizedRequest` only after all of the following succeed:

1. ordinary structural request checks;
2. capability schema and identity checks;
3. a well-formed validity interval with `expires_at > issued_at`;
4. subject binding;
5. action-scope binding;
6. parent/delegated/input authority ordering;
7. issuance/expiry checks at the supplied observation time;
8. revocation check.

Any failure is fail-closed and no `AuthorizedRequest` is constructed.

## Audit boundary

Capability audit records are append-only observations. They may record authorization or denial and its grant/request identifiers, but they are evidence only and cannot authorize, repair, widen, or revive a grant.

## Explicit nonclaims

v2.1 does not claim cryptographic signatures, cryptographic tamper resistance, distributed revocation persistence, external identity federation, autonomous delegation, provider correctness, or universal security. `RevocationSet` remains an in-process monotonic set unless a later artifact establishes durable persistence.

## Completion gate

A v2.1 candidate is eligible for sealing only after deterministic positive and negative tests cover the enforcement boundary, malformed validity windows, subject/scope/authority failures, expiry and revocation; the canonical locked NEXUS Verification succeeds on the exact candidate; exact-candidate evidence is bound; and a separately authorized release gate creates the immutable release.

No real verification -> no PASS -> no seal.
