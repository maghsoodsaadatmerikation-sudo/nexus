# Scientific reasoning review

The operator-authenticated POST /studio/api/research/reasoning-review accepts the original evidence request, matching evidence digest, a caller-classified claim kind, and optional source-indexed method annotations. Each annotation binds a nonempty excerpt span (Python Unicode code-point offsets) to its SHA-256. The service checks card type, span bounds, duplicate indices and prior evidence review. Metadata-only cards cannot receive method annotations. Encyclopedia cards are restricted to background/unknown. Caller-reported study designs, population match and limitations flags are not independently verified; an excerpt hash establishes exact input linkage, not entailment or correctness.

The rules report unresolved disagreement, unannotated support, missing applicability and limitations review, lack of reported experimental/quasi-experimental support for causal claims, and specific caveats for hypotheses, observations, associations and predictions. No numerical evidence score or automatic truth verdict is generated. Even a randomized-trial label never establishes causation. Systematic reviews are not assumed to contain causal designs. The service always requires human review, returns no raw excerpts, has no external dispatch, and does not make clinical recommendations. This is structured methodological screening, not general scientific reasoning, statistical inference, source authentication or systematic-review automation. No new dependencies, persistence or browser integration. Exact-head CI required.
