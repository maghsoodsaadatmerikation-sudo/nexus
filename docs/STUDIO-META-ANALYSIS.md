# Inverse-variance meta-analysis (v1)

Authenticated /studio/api/statistics/meta-analysis/review and /run accept 2–100 distinct caller-supplied study identifiers, finite effect estimates and positive standard errors, with explicit common effect scale (mean difference, standardized mean difference, log odds ratio or log risk ratio). Input effects are bounded to ±1e6, SE 1e-6–1e6. Log-scale inputs and outputs stay on the log scale; no transformation or conversion is inferred. Exact plan digest and explicit consent required. If independent estimates, comparable estimands and valid standard errors are reported, calculates common-effect inverse-variance and random-effects DerSimonian–Laird estimates, weights, normal approximation intervals, Cochran Q, I² and tau². C uses positive pair products to avoid cancellation under extreme weight imbalance. Missing assumptions withhold pooled results. Few studies, dominant weight and zero estimated heterogeneity are flagged. IDs are not evidence of independent study populations. No source retrieval, automatic effect conversion, bias correction, REML/Hartung–Knapp, prediction interval, causal/clinical conclusion, independent validation, network, persistence or UI. Normal intervals ignore uncertainty in heterogeneity estimates; not a complete systematic review. Tests include analytic cases and exact-head CI is required.
