# NEXUS Constitution

## Purpose

NEXUS exists to increase the quality, traceability, and inspectability of human judgment without transferring human meaning, identity, or final decision authority to a machine.

## Authority rules

- Human values and identity are human-owned.
- Machine analysis is non-authoritative.
- Evidence is not authority.
- Alternatives are not recommendations.
- A final judgment requires explicit human authorship.
- Execution requires constitutional authorization.
- Policy mutation is never an implicit side effect of analysis.

## Epistemic rules

Every material claim should preserve origin, provenance where available, and uncertainty.

Generated analysis must remain distinguishable from external evidence and human assertions.

## Verification rule

No real verification -> no claim of PASS -> no Seal.

A verification record must bind the verified revision, environment, lockfile state, verification log, and evidence bundle.

## Product rule

The UI may help a person clarify, compare, challenge, and record a decision. It must not silently convert an AI output into the person's judgment.

## Architectural rule

The gateway is a boundary adapter, not an authority engine. AI adapters produce epistemic objects, not execution authority.
