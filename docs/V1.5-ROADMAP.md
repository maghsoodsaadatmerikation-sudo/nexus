# NEXUS v1.5 Roadmap — Promotion Safety & Rollback Readiness

Status: **DEVELOPMENT / UNSEALED**

NEXUS v1.5 follows the sealed v1.4.0 deployment-truth boundary. It does not rewrite historical tags, release records, or provider observations.

## Constitutional invariant

`A_out <= A_in`

Production promotion is an execution act, not an inference. A verified release may be eligible for promotion, but verification alone must never be treated as authorization to mutate production.

## Goal

Define and enforce a fail-closed production-promotion contract that prevents NEXUS from claiming or performing a production cutover unless the exact target commit, durable-state boundary, rollback anchor, and provider-side post-cutover evidence are all explicit.

## Gates

### G1 — Historical release integrity
- `v1.4.0` must remain fixed to `dcf49302e8a8c4c935de3aad0585b19132bad2cd`.
- `docs/RELEASE-v1.4.0.md` remains historical and additive-only.

### G2 — Promotion target identity
- The only eligible production-promotion target for this stage is the exact sealed `v1.4.0` commit.
- Branch names, moving `main`, or documentation heads are not acceptable substitutes for an exact commit identity.

### G3 — Pre-cutover durable-state safety
- The observed production persistence boundary must remain `/data`.
- Production data must not be destructively tested as part of promotion readiness.
- The currently observed live commit is retained as the rollback anchor until new provider evidence supersedes it.

### G4 — Exact-SHA execution requirement
- A production promotion may occur only through a provider mechanism that can be bound to the exact intended commit or an equivalently immutable artifact identity.
- If the connected tooling cannot establish that binding, promotion must stop rather than approximate with a moving branch.

### G5 — Post-cutover evidence requirement
- A promotion is not considered complete until new provider-side evidence reports deployment status `SUCCESS`, the exact promoted commit, the durable `/data` mount, and no authority expansion.
- Release publication alone cannot satisfy this gate.

### G6 — Rollback readiness
- The pre-cutover live deployment identity must be recorded before mutation.
- Rollback must target an explicit previously observed deployment/artifact, never an inferred "last good" state.
- Automatic rollback is not authorized by verification CI.

### G7 — Operational economy
- No paid resource upgrade is required or authorized.
- No new service or volume is required for this stage.
- Existing auxiliary helpers remain outside the production authority path.

### G8 — v1.5 release gate
- The exact v1.5 candidate must pass NEXUS Verification.
- The promotion-safety contract and release-candidate manifest must pass fail-closed.
- v1.5 may be released as a verified promotion-safety protocol even if production promotion remains blocked by unavailable exact-SHA provider tooling.
- A v1.5 release does not itself authorize or imply a production cutover.
