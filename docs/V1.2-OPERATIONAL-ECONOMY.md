# NEXUS v1.2 G4 — Operational Economy

Status: IMPLEMENTED / AWAITING EXACT-COMMIT VERIFICATION

## Purpose

G4 ensures that v1.2 operational resilience does not silently become a requirement to purchase additional infrastructure or keep evidence-only helpers running continuously.

The constitutional invariant remains:

`A_out <= A_in`

Cost or infrastructure automation does not expand epistemic or execution authority.

## Provider state observed on 2026-09-14

Primary production service:

- `nexus-stage-d` remains the only service required for the live product path.
- Durable data volume remains mounted at `/data`.
- No paid resource upgrade was requested or performed as part of G4.

Auxiliary Stage D services:

- `stage-d-runner`
- `stage-d-operator`
- `nexus-stage-d-operator`
- `nexus-stage-d-restore`

These services are evidence/verification helpers, not production authority-bearing runtime components. Their restart policy is set to `NEVER` and sleeping mode is enabled so they are not intended to remain continuously active after evidence capture.

The connected Railway API layer currently rejects `numReplicas: 0` with a validation floor of one replica. G4 therefore does **not** claim that the provider configuration has a literal zero-replica value. It records the stronger claim actually supported by evidence: helpers are non-production, restart-disabled, sleep-enabled, and no paid scaling action was taken.

## Fail-closed cost rule

Progression must stop rather than silently incur cost if a future step requires any of the following:

- plan upgrade;
- paid storage expansion;
- additional paid replicas;
- paid region expansion;
- any other charge not already covered by the user's existing included resources.

No release gate may reinterpret the absence of a paid resource as permission to purchase one.

## Evidence preservation

G4 must not delete or mutate:

- the `/data` production volume;
- the `/evidence` witness volume;
- the v1.1 sealed release boundary;
- secrets;
- release evidence;
- the primary production service.

## Gate boundary

A G4 PASS means the repository's operational-economy contract is internally consistent, the production/evidence roles are separated, and the observed provider configuration does not depend on a newly purchased resource.

It does **not** claim a provider-wide billing guarantee, zero future charges, or that every provider-side meter is observable from NEXUS.
