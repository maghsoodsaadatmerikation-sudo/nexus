# NEXUS v1.5 Release Gate — Promotion Safety & Rollback Readiness

Status: **UNSEALED**

A v1.5 candidate is eligible for release publication only when all conditions below are satisfied.

## Required conditions

1. `scripts/v1.5-promotion-safety.sh` passes fail-closed on the exact candidate commit.
2. `v1.4.0` still resolves to `dcf49302e8a8c4c935de3aad0585b19132bad2cd`.
3. `docs/RELEASE-v1.4.0.md` and `evidence/live-deployment-state.json` remain present and internally consistent.
4. The provider observation still identifies `/data` as the durable persistence boundary and contains no authentication material.
5. The pre-cutover rollback anchor is explicit and equals the observed live deployment identity until superseded by new provider evidence.
6. Production promotion must be blocked unless an exact-SHA or equivalently immutable artifact binding is available for the existing primary service.
7. Moving branch names are not accepted as proof of deployment identity.
8. A promotion is not complete without new provider-side post-cutover evidence proving the exact promoted identity and `SUCCESS` state.
9. NEXUS Verification remains read-only with respect to production promotion and rollback.
10. No paid resource upgrade, new service, or new volume is required or authorized.
11. Authority expansion remains `NONE` and `A_out <= A_in` remains intact.
12. The exact v1.5 candidate commit must have a completed successful NEXUS Verification run before publication.

## Claim boundary

- **Promotion-ready** means the safety contract is complete and the intended target/rollback identities are explicit.
- **Promotion-executable** additionally requires a provider mechanism that can bind the production mutation to the exact target identity.
- **Promoted** requires new provider-side post-cutover evidence.

These states are not interchangeable.

## Human boundary

Release publication and production promotion are separate acts. A v1.5 release may seal the promotion-safety protocol while production remains deliberately unchanged. Verification automation may block unsafe promotion; it does not gain authority to perform or infer a production cutover.
