# Cross-claim conflict review

The authenticated POST /studio/api/research/conflict-review recomputes the original GraphRequest and checks its exact digest. Caller-supplied frames associate claim IDs with normalized proposition IDs, polarity and five scope identifiers (population, setting, period, measurement, conditions). Comparisons are limited to pairs sharing a proposition ID. Opposite polarity, the same claim kind and five explicitly matching scopes produce a potential conflict requiring review. Different scope identifiers mean reported difference, not proof of disjoint populations or compatibility. Missing scope is unresolved, even if missing on both sides. Same polarity is not corroboration. Opposite labels attached to a shared source are shown separately, not treated as logical contradiction.

No language-model extraction, semantic equivalence, contradiction proof, truth verdict, ranking, automatic deletion or resolution is performed. Identifiers and polarity are caller annotations, not validated interpretations of source prose. The service always requires human review; the digest binds input, not truth or approval. Up to six frames and existing graph limits apply, with 256 KiB body cap. No external execution, persistence or UI integration. Exact-head CI required.
