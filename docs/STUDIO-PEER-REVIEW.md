# Structured scientific peer-review checklist

Authenticated POST /studio/api/research/peer-review recomputes the exact manuscript review and requires its digest. Article-type-specific checklist: 14 criteria for empirical, nine for conceptual and commentary drafts. Caller annotations can report addressed, concern or not applicable with bounded rationale; addressed requires an exact nonblank manuscript span (Python Unicode code-point offsets, start inclusive/end exclusive). The report exposes hashes, not rationale or span text. All structural findings and unresolved research uncertainties remain visible, even when a criterion is reported addressed. Unreviewed, concern and not-applicable dispositions remain in the issue list. Stable issue identifiers identify structural findings/checklist criteria; they are not evidence that issues were resolved. The overall digest binds the original request. No automatic semantic review, independent methodology assessment, clinical advice, ethics/anonymity certification, plagiarism check, acceptance/rejection recommendation, journal fit, submission, provider call, storage or UI. Exact-head CI required.
